The chord-kind object

Type: String

Allowed values:

Value Description
"augmented" Triad: major third, augmented fifth.
"augmentedSeventh" Seventh: augmented triad, minor seventh.
"diminished" Triad: minor third, diminished fifth.
"diminishedSeventh" Seventh: diminished triad, diminished seventh.
"dominant" Seventh: major triad, minor seventh.
"dominant11th" 11th: dominant-ninth, perfect 11th.
"dominant13th" 13th: dominant-11th, major 13th.
"dominantNinth" Ninth: dominant, major ninth.
"french" Functional French sixth.
"german" Functional German sixth.
"halfDiminished" Seventh: diminished triad, minor seventh.
"italian" Functional Italian sixth.
"major" Triad: major third, perfect fifth.
"major11th" 11th: major-ninth, perfect 11th.
"major13th" 13th: major-11th, major 13th.
"majorMinor" Seventh: minor triad, major seventh.
"majorNinth" Ninth: major-seventh, major ninth.
"majorSeventh" Seventh: major triad, major seventh.
"majorSixth" Sixth: major triad, added sixth.
"minor" Triad: minor third, perfect fifth.
"minor11th" 11th: minor-ninth, perfect 11th.
"minor13th" 13th: minor-11th, major 13th.
"minorNinth" Ninth: minor-seventh, major ninth.
"minorSeventh" Seventh: minor triad, minor seventh.
"minorSixth" Sixth: minor triad, added sixth.
"none" Used to explicitly encode the absence of chords or functional harmony.
"other" Used when the harmony is entirely composed of add elements.
"power" Perfect fifth.
"suspendedFourth" Suspended: perfect fourth, perfect fifth.
"suspendedSecond" Suspended: major second, perfect fifth.
"tristan" Augmented fourth, augmented sixth, augmented ninth.
